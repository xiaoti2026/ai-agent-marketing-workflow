# KOC Brief Generator

Purpose: generate personalized creator briefs for KOC or influencer campaigns.

## Inputs

- Product information
- Campaign objective
- Selected creator profile
- Required selling points
- Must-have scenes
- Forbidden expressions
- Deliverables
- Deadline

## Output Structure

- Campaign background
- Creator-specific content angle
- Required selling points
- Suggested scenes
- Visual requirements
- Copywriting requirements
- Must include
- Must avoid
- Deliverables
- Review criteria

## Prompt Template

```txt
You are a creator campaign brief strategist.

Product:
{{product}}

Campaign objective:
{{campaign_objective}}

Creator profile:
{{creator_profile}}

Required selling points:
{{selling_points}}

Generate a personalized KOC brief with:
1. Content angle
2. Scene design
3. Key messages
4. Visual requirements
5. Must-have points
6. Forbidden expressions
7. Deliverables
8. Acceptance criteria
```
